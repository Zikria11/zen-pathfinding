⚠ Speed

BRA* is 7× slower than A* on average. On 1024 maps it can exceed 20 seconds (London, Boston). The overhead grows super-linearly with map size.

⚠ Reliability

BRA* fails on 11 / 56 instances — including maps where A*, BFS, and Dijkstra all succeed. It times out or exhausts search space on Berlin-2-256, Milan-0-256, Paris-2-256, Boston-0-256, London-1-256, and all Moscow/Berlin no-path maps.

✓ Path Quality

On successful runs, BRA* finds paths only 5% longer than optimal (A* / BFS baseline). Far better than DFS (+2800%), Greedy (+15%), or Weighted A* (+8%).

✓ Risk Score

BRA* achieves the lowest average risk score (103.9) among successful algorithms — even beating ZEN (115.6) and REST A* (103.9). Good for safety-critical routing.
